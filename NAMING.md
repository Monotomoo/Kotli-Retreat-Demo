## 4. The Mirna collision — eyes open

- **Hotel Mirna – Terme & Wellness LifeClass, Portorož (SLO)**: 4★, 81 rooms, big DACH marketing. ~40 km away but a *coastal mass spa hotel* vs. your *private village retreat in interior Istria* — different product, different search intent.
- Several **"Villa Mirna"** holiday rentals across Istria (Svetvinčenat, Pazin, Medulin) — generic noise, no brand.
- Nobody operates a "**Mirna Retreat**". The exact-match domain space is empty.

**Mitigation:** always use the two-word lockup **"Mirna Retreat"** (never "Hotel Mirna" pattern), anchor SEO on "Mirna Retreat Kotli / Istria interior", and register word+figurative trademark later. The name being the *actual river at the property* is also a genuine-use defense.

**Why it still wins:** it's the only candidate scoring 10 on place-soul while staying effortless in German AND meaning something ("peaceful") — the Raus/Unyoked single-word emotional pattern, but authentically Croatian.

### 10c. The one real objection to it

*"Villages" is a promise about what you buy.* Kotli is a village. Property #2 might be a mill, a farmstead, an old school, a stone villa, a lighthouse — none of which are villages. The umbrella would then either constrain acquisitions or quietly become inaccurate.

Two honest resolutions:
- **Accept it as strategy** — "we revive Croatia's abandoned villages" is a *sharper* thesis than "we buy rural property," and sharp theses get written about. The constraint is the brand.
- **Or hedge with a broader umbrella** → 10d.

## 11. ROUND 5 — THE VENUE: FINAL POOL (2026-07-22)

Umbrella settled (§10: Quiet Villages, Hinterland hedge). This is the last open question: **what exactly is the venue called?**

### 11a. Two findings that decide it

**1. Kotli already has organic search traffic — you inherit it.**
The Kotli waterfall is an established Istrian attraction: a [TripAdvisor listing](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g15247341-d553276-Reviews-or20-Kotli_Waterfall-Kotli_Istria.html), a stop on Buzet's **Seven Waterfalls Trail**, and press calling it ["Croatia's Pamukkale"](https://www.luxurylivingcroatia.com/posts/kotli-istria) — 12 m falls, turquoise pools, an abandoned 19th-century stone village empty for 40+ years. **An invented name starts at zero; a Kotli name starts with people already searching.** This is the single strongest argument for keeping "Kotli" in the brand.

**2. But bare "Kotli" is unwinnable in search.** [Kotli, Azad Kashmir, Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotli_District) is a district of **774,194 people** — with its own river and waterfalls. You will never outrank it for the single word. → **Always ship "Kotli" with a qualifier.** This kills the bare-wordmark option and confirms the two-word lockup.

**Confirmed brand story:** *kotli* comes from Croatian **kotao — "cauldron."** The river hollowed cauldron-shaped basins into the stone, and the village took their name. Your swimming pools named the village. That's the origin line for the About page. *(River note: the Rečina flows through Kotli and becomes the Mirna downstream; sources differ on which name applies at the village — verify locally before printing it.)*
